# Cherry MX Black ABS — typing samples

Per-key mechanical keyboard recordings, optimized for zero-latency typing in Lettera.

- **Source pack:** Cherry MX Black ABS by mechvibes, distributed through
  [thock-soundpacks](https://github.com/kamillobinski/thock-soundpacks) (Thock folder `DE401889-0CCB-4522-A213-836AAD88C447`)
- **Upstream project:** [Mechvibes](https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes)
- **License:** MIT

## Processing applied

1. **The key-down strike only**: each original holds the press and, about 110 ms later, the release click, cut off by the end of the file. Each sample starts at the press (walking back to 4% of its peak, 2.0 ms pre-roll), keeps its whole natural decay and ends at the quietest point before the release, with a 12 ms cosine fade (0.5 ms fade-in; at most 200.0 ms).
2. **Keys**: as the pack maps them, by PC scancode; the ISO key next to Z sounds like the backslash key.
3. **Downmixed to mono and normalized to 0.9 peak**.

Result: 84 samples, total size 566.5 KB (reduced from 2801.6 KB).
