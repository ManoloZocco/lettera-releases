# Cherry MX Brown PBT — typing samples

Per-key mechanical keyboard recordings, optimized for zero-latency typing in Lettera.

- **Source pack:** `3AA3C556-AB47-4AB9-A791-498BF9910BF7`
- **Upstream author:** mechvibes (Mechvibes / Thock)
- **License:** MIT

## Processing applied

1. **Trimmed to the strike**: Each sample was trimmed to the attack onset (walking back to 4% of peak amplitude with 2.0 ms pre-roll), eliminating tens of milliseconds of initial room tone/silence.
2. **Micro-fade & decay**: Applied 0.5 ms cosine fade-in and 8.0 ms fade-out over natural decay (capped at 200.0 ms).
3. **Downmixed to mono and normalized to 0.9 peak**.

Result: 84 samples, total size 1051.7 KB (reduced from 2675.8 KB).
